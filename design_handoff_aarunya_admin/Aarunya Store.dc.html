About us

A little jewellery box from Kerala

Aarunya Jewels by Veena began at home, with a simple wish: jewellery that looks lovely, feels light and stays bright through everyday wear.

Every piece is picked by Veena and is anti-tarnish, so you can wear it to college, to work, to a cousin’s wedding, or simply because you feel like it.

Aarunya is the little girl in our story: a small heart with a big love for beautiful things.

With love, Veena ♡
